Space video sonification

Hear the universe, one star at a time

Galaxy Harmonix listens to Hubble footage frame by frame. Bright stars become notes, the colour of the nebula becomes a drone, and the result is a soundtrack generated entirely from the pixels.

Sonify a video How it works

The pipeline

From pixels to sound in three steps

01

Scan

Each second, one frame is sampled and two square rings around its centre are split into 10×10 pixel boxes.

02

Map

Boxes with pixels above your brightness threshold become notes: more bright pixels, higher pitch. Average redness sets a low drone.

03

Synthesise

Notes are built from sine-wave overtones shaped by an ADSR envelope, mixed, and muxed back onto the video with ffmpeg.
